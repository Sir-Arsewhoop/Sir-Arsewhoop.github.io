This post exists to be copied. Its frontmatter is the shape every post takes, and
the colon in its title is deliberate — an unquoted `title:` containing a colon is
the single likeliest reason a post silently fails to publish.

Ordinary markdown works as you'd expect: **bold**, _italic_, `inline code`, links,
lists and images.

- Drop a new file into `_posts/` named `YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md`.
- Commit it. GitHub builds and deploys.
- That is the whole process.

> There is no build step you have to run, and no toolchain you need installed.
